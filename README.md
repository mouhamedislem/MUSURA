# MESURA — Fashion Workshop Management System

> A modern management application developed in C++ and Qt for managing the activities of a fashion workshop.

---

## 📌 Overview

**MESURA** is a management application designed to centralize and simplify the management of a fashion workshop.

The application provides a unified platform where different aspects of the workshop can be managed through dedicated modules. Each team member is responsible for developing one management module, which will then be integrated into the complete application.

The project focuses on creating a **modern, intuitive, and professional user experience** while maintaining a modular and maintainable software architecture.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of MESURA are to:

- Centralize fashion workshop management.
- Simplify day-to-day management operations.
- Provide an intuitive and modern desktop interface.
- Improve organization and accessibility of information.
- Facilitate searching, filtering, and data management.
- Automate repetitive management tasks.
- Provide a modular architecture for easier development and maintenance.
- Integrate all workshop management modules into one application.

---

## 🧩 Management Modules

MESURA is divided into **six main management modules**, with each module developed by a member of the project team.

| Module | Description |
|---|---|
| 👤 **Gestion des utilisateurs** | Management of users, accounts, roles, permissions, and user-related information. |
| 📦 **Gestion des commandes** | Management and tracking of workshop orders. |
| 👗 **Gestion des créations** | Management of fashion creations and related information. |
| 🧵 **Gestion des fournitures** | Management of supplies, materials, and available resources. |
| 👥 **Gestion des clients** | Management of customers and their information. |
| ⚙️ **Gestion des machines** | Management and monitoring of workshop machines and equipment. |

Each module is developed independently before being integrated into the complete MESURA application.
